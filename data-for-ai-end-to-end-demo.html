LPI Risk Factors

Primary Risk Indicators

  • Payment Processing Time: >30 days
  • Prior Delay History: 2+ instances
  • Claim Complexity: High (multi-line)
  • Clinical Review Required: Yes
  • Authorization Status: Pending
  • Documentation Issues: Missing attachments

Secondary Risk Indicators

  • Provider Payment Velocity: Slow
  • Claim Amount Anomaly: Unusual amount
  • Member Eligibility Status: Borderline
  • Policy Benefit Utilization: High %
  • Prior Appeals: High frequency
  • Network Participation: Non-contracted

Fraud Risk Indicators

Provider-Level Signals

  • Bill Submission: Bulk submissions, unusual patterns
  • Service Frequency: Exceptionally high utilization
  • Coding Patterns: Unusual diagnosis/procedure combinations
  • Billing Amount: Significantly higher than peers
  • Geographic Anomaly: Services outside service area
  • Credential Status: Inactive/excluded providers

Member-Level Signals

  • Service Concentration: Multiple same-day services
  • Unusual Utilization: Inconsistent with history
  • Network Usage: Out-of-network provider patterns
  • Authorization Gaps: Missing prior auth documentation
  • Claim Timing: Unusual submission delays
  • Collusion Indicators: Links to high-risk providers
